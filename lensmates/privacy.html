Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Lensmates is a personal, non-commercial app: a shared disposable camera for events. This page explains what it collects, where that data goes and how to delete it.

What Lensmates collects

  • A random account ID. Joining an event signs you in anonymously. There is no email, phone number or password for guests.
  • The name and doodle face you pick, shown to others in the same event.
  • The photos you take in an event, with the time each was taken and its size.
  • A push notification token, only if you allow notifications, so the app can tell you when the roll unlocks.
  • Points picks and votes, if the event uses them: which mate you backed and with how many points, and which photos you voted for. Points are just numbers for fun. Nothing is bought, paid or cashed out.
  • The host's email address, used only for the host to sign in.

Lensmates does not collect your location, contacts or any photos you didn't take in the app. It has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Where it is stored

  • Account, event and photo records are stored with Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India).
  • Photos are stored in the event host's own Google Photos account, in an album the app creates for that event.

Who can see your photos

Only people who joined the same event, and only after the event's unlock time. Before unlock, nobody can see them, including you. The host can hide or remove photos.

Google user data

Lensmates connects to the host's Google Photos with the Google Photos Library API. It uses that access only to create one album per event, add the event's photos to it, and read back the photos the app itself added so event members can view them. It cannot see or change any other photos or albums in that Google account.

Data from Google is not sold, not shared with anyone outside the event, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Lensmates' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The host can remove Lensmates' access at any time from Google account connections.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: to take shots in an event.
  • Photos: only when you choose to save a photo to your phone.
  • Notifications: optional, for the unlock reminder.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete my data. This deletes your account, your name and face, your event memberships, your photo records and your notification token, your points picks and your votes from the server, and clears shots still waiting on your phone. If an event's results were already final, its leaderboard keeps the numbers but shows you as "Someone who left".

Google doesn't let apps delete photos from Google Photos, so copies already uploaded stay in the host's Google Photos until the host deletes them there.

Children

Lensmates is not meant for children under 13.
