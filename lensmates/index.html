Lensmates

See the event through everyone else's eyes.

Lensmates is a shared disposable camera for trips, weddings and parties. Everyone shoots on their own phone, nobody sees a picture until the morning after, and then the whole roll opens for everyone at once.

How it works

  1. The host creates an event and shares a QR code or link.
  2. Friends join with no account or email. They pick a name and a doodle face.
  3. Everyone gets 10, 20 or 36 shots, with no preview.
  4. At unlock time (9 AM the next morning by default) the roll opens for everyone.

Where the photos go

Photos are stored in the event host's own Google Photos, in an album the app creates for each event. Lensmates only touches the albums and photos it created itself.

Lensmates is a personal, non-commercial project and is not in the App Store or Play Store.
